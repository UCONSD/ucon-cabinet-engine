## A1 — may a 1178 hood liner sit over a 1219 range?

**Asked because:** 545 Avenida Primavera has a 48" `DF48650C/S/P` (1219 wide) and the hood
is to be **built into the cabinetry above it**, which means a Pro hood liner rather than a
wall hood. **No liner is 1219.** printed p.142 gives five widths — 873, 1026, 1178, 1330,
1483 — and the two either side of the range are 1178 (41 narrower, ~20 each side) and 1330.

**What the guide says, and about what.** printed p.144:

> *"**Wall hoods** should be at least as wide as the cooking surface and **island hoods**
> should be 3" (76) wider on each side."*

It names wall hoods and island hoods. **It does not name liners**, and the liner paragraph on
the same page talks only about the decorative hood's material and the bottom plane. A liner is
not a wall hood, so this sentence is not evidence either way.

**Why it cannot be reasoned out.** The liner widths are all ⅜" sizes — 34⅜, 40⅜, 46⅜ — which
look designed to sit INSIDE a decorative hood built to a nominal size, so a 46⅜ liner may well
be Wolf's intended part for a nominal 48" hood. That is a plausible reading of a pattern, and
this project has been wrong before on exactly that move: the 120 mm fridge base closed to the
millimetre and was false.

**What it blocks.** 1330 does not fit the 1220 the cabinets occupy, so choosing it means
widening the cabinet above the range and taking width from its neighbours — a change to the
whole south row. 1178 fits with 21 mm each side. **Nothing is drawn or encoded until this is
answered.**

**Andriy, 2026-08-28: ask Wolf.**

## A2 — does the charbroiler depth recommendation reach a LINER at all?

**Asked because:** printed p.144 says

> *"For optimal performance in wall hood applications, a Pro **27" deep** wall hood is
> recommended for use with ranges and rangetops with a charbroiler or griddle."*

`DF48650C/S/P` **is** the charbroiler model — its own product name is *48" Dual Fuel Range —
6 Burners and Infrared Charbroiler* — and `48_Core` specifies `PW482418`, the **24"** deep
hood. So the set as written already sits on the wrong side of that sentence.

**But the kitchen is now taking a liner, and a liner has no 24"/27" choice at all**: printed
p.142 gives liner depths as **19⅜" (492)** and **22⅝" (575)**, both shallower than either
wall-hood depth the recommendation compares. The sentence is phrased in the wall hood's own
vocabulary and there is no way to satisfy or violate it with a liner.

**So the question is what the recommendation is really about** — the hood's projection over
the cooking surface, or its capture volume, or its CFM — because whichever it is decides what
the liner equivalent would be, if there is one. printed p.146–149 hold the CFM and blower
tables per model and have **not been read**.

**Status: open, and recorded rather than acted on.** It is a specification question for Andriy
and the client as much as for Wolf.
