## `Extraction Limitations`\
\
| Limitation | Source impact | Planning consequence |\
|---|---|---|\
| The source tables contain pricing columns numbered 1-11, but the finish-group definitions were not part of this extraction. | A visible number cannot safely be assigned to a named price group from this section alone. | No pricing or commercial comparison should be performed from this extract. |\
| Source dimensions are generally presented in centimeters, including decimal USA widths such as 45.7, 61, 76.2 and 91.4. | Values were converted to 457, 610, 762 and 914 mm. | Conversion is suitable for preliminary geometry but does not establish manufacturing tolerance. |\
| Several tables combine codes, widths, depths, front heights and pricing in dense visual layouts. | Parsed text does not always preserve the original column relationship. | Rows with uncertain code-to-dimension mapping are marked unclear or needs Elda confirmation. |\
| \'93Tall unit for oven H.60\'94 appears repeatedly. | The source does not explicitly identify this as a final appliance cutout of exactly 600 mm. | Use a preliminary appliance placeholder only; do not issue an appliance approval based on H.60. |\
| \'93Tall oven and microwave/double oven unit\'94 is a combined source description. | It does not define which appliance pairs, sizes or brands are permitted. | Do not infer microwave, speed-oven or double-oven compatibility. |\
| Refrigerator entries are predominantly described as doors or fronts. | The section does not consistently identify complete refrigerator carcasses. | Do not model the front as a complete refrigerator cabinet. Use a separate appliance placeholder. |\
| Refrigerator front heights are shown as segmented diagram values on several pages. | Complete segmentation-to-code mapping is not always recoverable from extracted text alone. | Detailed page-by-page visual extraction is required before component production. |\

| No platform unit was clearly identified in the extracted USA Elements pages. | A platform component cannot be source-confirmed. | Exclude platforms from v0.1 unless Elda identifies the applicable source item. |\
| No standalone filler profile was clearly identified. A fixed filler is included within specific oven-unit configurations. | The fixed filler cannot be generalized as a standard filler-profile component. | Model only as an unresolved part of the specific cabinet configuration. |\
| This is a preliminary planning extraction. | It is not a production order schedule. | Every selected unit and front requires Cesar/DzineElements technical review. |\
