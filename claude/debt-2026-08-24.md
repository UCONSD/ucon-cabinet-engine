## 5. What the numbers do not say

The mapped remainder is **not** 1 230 codes of transcription. Every section
opened so far has had the same three-part shape — a clean page or two, then
interior mechanisms the catalog names and does not code, then corners carrying
the D/S letter, then appliance housings. Group 2 was estimated at ~232 codes and
gave **72** of reading; the rest joined two standing blockers.

So the real queue is shorter than 1 230 and the blocker list is the thing that
governs it:

1. **`registry/cesar/options/`** — printed p.568-569, the kit catalog. **Five
   positions in five chapters point at it.** The most referenced unread page in
   the registry.
2. **Elda Q7b** — the D/S execution letter, seen in five chapters.
3. **The appliance module** — every oven, microwave and fridge housing.
4. **The door-version axis** — 19 held codes cannot be drawn until it narrows
   from a family to an article.
5. **The collection axis (M3)** — 1 260 codes behind it.
