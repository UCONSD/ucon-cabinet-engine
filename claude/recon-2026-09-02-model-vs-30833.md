### The join, and what the strict tolerance proved by failing

Spec §2 asks for a 1 mm match on the `L / H / P` triple. Run as written it
matched **34 of 69 rows** and refused the rest — and the refusal is the finding,
not a tolerance to relax:

**HER `P` COLUMN AND OURS DO NOT MEAN THE SAME THING.** For a filler, a front or
a panel she prints the **depth of the run the piece belongs to** — `C00151` at
620, `V80630` at 620, `PD0151` at 350 — where we record the piece's **own
thickness**, 22 or 18. Her row 1 and our body #27 are the same filler; on the
triple they are 49×2340×**620** against 50×2340×**22** and they never meet.

So the join is on the **FACE** — the two largest dimensions — for anything whose
smallest dimension is a thickness, and on the full triple for a carcass. With
that, every row that has a body found one. **The tolerance was not widened**; the
deltas are printed in the table and three of them are the substance of §2.
