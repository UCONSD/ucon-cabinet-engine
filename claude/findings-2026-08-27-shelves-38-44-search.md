# A horizontal shelf 38-44 mm thick — searched across all five volumes (2026-08-27)

**Volumes searched, all of them, by their own text layers:** Project Guidelines
(300 pp), Kitchen System (634), Linear Elements (244), Home Elements (176),
Bathroom Collection (202). Every statement of the form *"N cm thick"* in all five
was collected and bucketed; the whole distribution is below, so an absence here
is a real absence and not an unread page.

## 1. The answer in one line

**Nothing in the SHELVES chapter is in that band. The 4,0 cm you want is a
BREAKFAST BAR / LIVING TOP, and the wall hardware for it exists and names the
thickness.**

## 2. What the Shelves chapter actually sells — Volume 3, printed p.221-229

| printed | what | thicknesses |
|---|---|---|
| 223 | Shelves, per lm | **2,2** |
| 224 | Shelves, per lm and per m² | **6,0** |
| 225-227 | Shelves with back panel | back 1,8 · shelf **2,2** |
| 229 | Dressup Line shelf D.16 | 1,6 |

**Two thicknesses, and 38-44 falls between them.** Not a gap in the reading — the
pages print 2,2 and 6,0 and nothing else.

**The code grammar, decoded from the tables** (worth keeping, it is not written
anywhere): `MNS` + **thickness** ×10 in three digits + **depth** in cm, three
digits. `MNS022038` = 2,2 thick, D.38. `MNS060060` = 6,0 thick, D.60.
`MNS060000` = 6,0, sold **per m²** rather than per lm, at D.120.

## 3. Where 4,0 cm lives

The whole distribution of stated thicknesses across the five books:

    0,1 0,3 0,4 0,6 1 1,2 1,3 1,4 1,6 1,8 2 2,2 2,5 2,8 2,9 3 3,1 3,2
    3,6 **4** 4,6 5,2 5,5 6 8 12 18 20 21,5 24 28

In the 3,5-4,5 band there are exactly two values: **3,6** (Volume 3 p.202-203)
and **4** — and the 4 is on:

- **Volume 3 printed p.10** — *"HPL … Available **2.2 and 4 cm thick**"*, and the
  page's own cross-section note: **"2.2/4 cm thick tops"**, depths
  **38 / 65 / 70 / 75 / 80** and 103/108/128.
- **Volume 3 printed p.28** — *Breakfast bar and Living top finishes*, **2,2 and 4**.
- **Volume 3 printed p.29-31** — *Living top finishes*, 1,3 / 3 / **4**.
- **Volume 3 printed p.32** — *Breakfast bar and Living top finishes*, **4** and 6.
- Volume 3 p.21 and p.23, and Volume 1 p.270-293 (the finishes repeats).

So in this system **a 40 mm horizontal board is a top, not a shelf**, and the
depth range it is sold in — from 38 cm — is shelf-shaped.

## 4. And the wall hardware names the thickness, which settles it

Volume 3 printed p.228, *Accessories for shelves*:

| what | code | points | the words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concealed shelf support for walls | **`990316`** | 16 | **"for 4/6 cm th. – each"** · max 35 cm deep · **2 every 100 cm** |
| … the 2,2 version | `990315` | 16 | "for 2.2 cm th. – each" |
| … into 4/6 thick plaster | `990317` | 16 | |
| Concealed shelf support for back panel | `990307` | 23 | for 2.2 cm th. |
| **L-shaped bracket for breakfast bar** | **`990331`** | 61 | laminate, lacquered and veneered bars only · onto a **4-6 cm partition** · **max depth 38 cm** · 2 every 100 cm |

**A concealed support made for a 4 cm board is the catalog telling us a 4 cm
board gets hung on a wall.** That is the floating shelf.

## 5. Modifiable — yes, and here is the list

Tops and shelves alike are sold **per linear metre or per m² and cut**. Volume 3
printed p.224 prints the surcharges: **out-of-square reduction · internal or
external cutout · shaping of hood chimney · angled or rounded front edge** (the
last "check for feasibility and request a quotation" for Fenix, Unicolor and
stainless steel).

## 6. What was looked at and ruled out

- **Volume 2 p.573-575** — shelves matching the carcass finish, and glass
  shelves with supports: **0,8 cm**. Interior shelves, not floating.
- **Volume 2 p.455-456** — open base/wall/tall units, 2,2. Boxes, not boards.
- **Volume 2 p.457-468** — Thin. 1,8/1,4 members; already held and stubbed.
- **Volume 4** — THE 50's wall system (p.13-35) and DressUp Wall Solution
  (p.74-93). Shelf systems, but their boards are 1,3 and their glass 1,0.
- **Volume 5** — nothing; the shelf mentions are interior fittings of base units.

## 7. The two limits to design against

- **35 cm** — the depth the concealed wall supports are recommended to.
- **38 cm** — the maximum depth of a bracket-mounted breakfast bar, and also the
  shallowest top depth the chapter prints. The two numbers meeting at 38 is
  probably not a coincidence and has not been checked.
